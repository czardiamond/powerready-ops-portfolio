A proposed operations concept

PowerReady Ops

A proposed 30-day operational continuity pilot to help a Nigerian small business prepare its workflows for electricity interruptions.

Proposed pilot · not yet launched

Why this concept?

The survey establishes that many firms experienced outages. PowerReady Ops is a proposed way to organize the business workflows around that challenge—not a claim that outages or their effects have been solved.

The proposed approach

Turn disruption into a documented response.

PowerReady Ops is a non-technical operations-support concept. The pilot would help one SME map critical routines, assign next steps, keep a simple incident record, and coordinate with the client’s chosen qualified technical providers when needed.

01 · MAP

Critical workflows

Identify which tasks, tools, and customer commitments need a continuity plan.

02 · PREPARE

Simple routines

Document who does what during an interruption, using client-approved procedures.

03 · COORDINATE

Clear handoffs

Organize contacts and requests for qualified maintenance or backup-power providers.

04 · REVIEW

Useful records

Log incidents and review the data with the business to refine its procedures.

Possible pilot outputs

Practical tools the team can keep using.

Continuity playbook
Key workflows, roles, and client-approved response steps.
Outage and downtime log
A simple, consistent record for operational review.
Readiness checklist
Routine checks for contacts, essential supplies, and agreed preparations.
Communication templates
Draft staff and customer updates for the business to review and approve.
Provider coordination sheet
Contact and escalation details for the client’s chosen qualified providers.
Pilot review
A short summary of observations and proposed next improvements.

Suggested structure

A proposed 30-day pilot.

Scope, timing, and measures would be agreed with a participating SME before the pilot begins.

Days 1–7 · Understand

Map & baseline

  • Map selected workflows
  • Agree roles and escalation contacts
  • Start a baseline incident log

Days 8–25 · Test

Use & adjust

  • Introduce the playbook and checklists
  • Record interruptions and response steps
  • Coordinate agreed provider handoffs

Days 26–30 · Review

Learn & decide

  • Review the recorded measures
  • Revise the proposed procedures
  • Decide whether further support is useful

Measures to agree: outage count and duration, affected workflows, delayed tasks, checklist completion, and response/communication steps recorded. No savings or improvement is assumed in advance.
