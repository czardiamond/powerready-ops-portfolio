# PowerReady Ops | Diamond Anyanwu

A single-page portfolio describing a **proposed 30-day operational continuity pilot** for Nigerian SMEs. The concept is not a launched service and makes no claim of proven outcomes.

## Local preview

Open `index.html` in a browser or serve this directory with a static HTTP server.

## Evidence

The website reports that 82.5% of surveyed Nigerian firms experienced electrical outages in 2025. The World Bank Enterprise Survey page states that it interviewed 1,043 firms from February through September 2025.

- [World Bank Enterprise Survey: Nigeria 2025](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data/exploreeconomies/2025/nigeria)
- [World Bank indicator API: IC.ELC.OUTG.ZS](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/NGA/indicator/IC.ELC.OUTG.ZS?format=json)

## Scope boundary

PowerReady Ops is an operational-support concept. Electrical assessments, installation, repairs, and power-system work are outside its scope and are referred to appropriately qualified providers.
